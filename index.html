Key State Sentiment Highlights for Mizoram

Public trust in Mizoram Police remains high (78.6%) boosted by community-supported narcotics seizures. Sentiments regarding Assam Rifles (63.4%) reflect strong appreciation for local medical and disaster assistance, balanced against vocal civil opposition to physical Indo-Myanmar border fencing and demands to accelerate headquarters transfer to Zokhawsang. Indian Army enjoys high admiration (77.1%) with heavy youth turnout for Agniveer recruitment.
